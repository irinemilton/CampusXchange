Values & Community

CampusXchange is a decentralized barter platform designed for students to exchange academic resources using virtual credits.

1. List Your Items

Have an old textbook or lab coat? List it on the marketplace. Set a fair credit value and category to help others find it.

2. Engage in Barter

Browse the marketplace for what you need. Request an exchange and use your virtual credits to acquire resources.

3. Build Your Credits

Every successful exchange earns you credits. Rank up on the leaderboard and become a top contributor in your campus community.

Our Vision for Campus Economy

We believe that academic resources should stay within the campus ecosystem. By removing the dependency on cash and focusing on virtual credits, we promote sustainability and resource accessibility for every student.
